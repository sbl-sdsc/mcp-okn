## Prompt

Conduct a reproducible, multi-knowledge-graph integrative study of Type 2 Diabetes (T2D) to generate a comprehensive, evidence-backed map of its molecular biology, genetics, clinical manifestations, epidemiology, and therapeutic landscape.
Follow the `/okn-bioanalysis` workflow and produce the final report using `/okn-report-style`.
Objectives

1. Characterize Type 2 Diabetes and its subtypes
   * Identify the major forms, subtypes, and clinical classifications of Type 2 Diabetes.
   * Account for synonymous disease names, ontology mappings, and identifier crosswalks across all knowledge graphs.
   * Document differences in disease definitions, diagnostic criteria, and ontology coverage where applicable.
2. Integrate evidence across knowledge graphs
   * Search broadly across all relevant knowledge graphs available through mcp-okn.
   * Harmonize identifiers for genes, variants, pathways, diseases, drugs, biomarkers, tissues, and phenotypes.
   * Use cross-graph joins whenever they provide complementary evidence.
   * Document which knowledge graphs contributed to each finding.
3. Identify molecular mechanisms
   * Construct a comprehensive molecular profile organized by entity type, including:
      * protein-coding genes
      * non-coding RNAs (kept separate)
      * genetic variants
      * pathways and gene sets
      * biological processes
      * molecular functions
      * upstream regulators
   * Rank genes, variants, and pathways by the strength and consistency of evidence across sources.
4. Characterize disease-associated molecular activity
   * Identify genes with altered expression or activity in Type 2 Diabetes.
   * Record:
      * tissue or organ
      * cell type
      * direction of change (upregulated/downregulated)
      * experimental context
      * supporting datasets
   * Highlight genes consistently dysregulated across multiple tissues or studies.
5. Therapeutic landscape
   * Identify approved drugs, investigational therapeutics, biologics, and emerging treatment targets.
   * Associate drugs with their molecular targets, pathways, mechanisms of action, and supporting evidence.
   * Identify opportunities for drug repurposing suggested by cross-graph evidence.
6. Clinical features and biomarkers
   * Identify:
      * clinical phenotypes
      * diagnostic biomarkers
      * prognostic biomarkers
      * disease complications
      * laboratory measurements
   * Rank biomarkers according to evidence strength and clinical utility.
7. Epidemiology and population health
   * Characterize the prevalence of Type 2 Diabetes across geographic regions.
   * Integrate prevalence data with:
      * demographic variables
      * socioeconomic indicators
      * environmental factors
      * social determinants of health
   * Identify statistically supported correlations and geographic patterns.
   * Generate a prevalence map highlighting regional variation.
8. Evidence assessment
   * For every reported relationship, record separately:
      * supporting knowledge graph(s)
      * relationship type
      * confidence or effect size (when available)
      * evidence type:
         * curated knowledge
         * statistical association
         * genetic association
         * differential molecular activity
         * pathway membership
         * literature-derived evidence
   * Preserve these evidence categories independently rather than merging them into a single confidence score.
9. Consensus analysis
   * Rank findings according to cross-source agreement.
   * Highlight the highest-confidence consensus set.
   * Identify conflicting evidence, knowledge gaps, ontology mismatches, and likely underrepresented areas.

### 3. Consensus, scoring and tiers
- **Universe** = 2,117 genes with ≥1 disease-anchored stream. **Core** = 381 genes with ≥2.
- `n_disease_streams` = (pigean_weight present) + (spokeokn_DaG) + (n_gwas_variants>0) + (prokn_clinvar). Max 4; observed 1:1736, 2:320, 3:52, 4:9.
- **Integrated score** = 3.0·ev_curated + 2.5·ev_genetic + 2.0·clip(pigean_weight/10·2, 0, 2) + 1.5·ev_diffexpr + 1.0·ev_qtl + 1.5·ev_druggable + 1.0·ev_aop + 0.5·ev_exposure, with ev_curated ∈ {0,1,2} (prokn ClinVar + spoke-okn) and ev_diffexpr ∈ {0,1,2} (GXA + pankgraph OCR).

### 8. Limitations that constrain replication
1. spoke-okn has NO T2D node — all spoke-okn disease-gene edges and ALL epidemiology are **all-cause diagnosed diabetes**.
2. oard-kg has no MONDO T2D term (HP:0005978 instead) and is a rare-disease EHR resource: 27 of 28 MONDO diabetes-complication terms have zero associations.
3. `DOID:9352` is a leaf in the loaded ubergraph slice (no subclasses, no label).
4. pankgraph has no `Type2Diabetic__expression_*` predicate — its donor-group differential expression is T1D-only.
5. spoke-okn has zero diabetes mortality records and only 163 `TREATS_CtD` edges federation-wide (no antidiabetic drug).
6. prokn `RO:0002212` / `RO:0002213` (LINCS transcriptional perturbation) uses PubChem CIDs, disjoint from the ChEMBL IRIs carrying indications — the two layers do not join.
7. Variant-to-gene attribution is nearest-gene in the source graphs (ANKH vs ANK1; TLE1 vs TLE4 — both pairs present as distinct genes with distinct rsIDs).
8. County-level results are ecological; the PM2.5 null is a plausible artefact of county-mean exposure.
