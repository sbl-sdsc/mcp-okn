2. Cohort — verified & cross-study reproducibility

Seven NASA OSDR mouse eye studies, Space-Flight-vs-Ground-Control (direction rule) with the comparability rule applied; rebuilt live to exactly match the verified anchors. OSD-758/759 carry four distinct-gravity arms — the microgravity (uG) arm is the primary contrast.

3. Fluid-shift attribution & microgravity-vs-radiation

Ground fluid-shift analog (OSD-203 hindlimb unloading). The loading main effect (non-irradiated) reproduces only 27/878 (≈2%) of the flight-retina signature; where they overlap, ρ=0.63 (p<0.001) but the overlap is a muscle/metabolic module and the oxidative MT1 program is reversed.
On-orbit 1G-centrifugation control. Restoring gravity in flight collapses the retinal response 878→46 genes (7 shared, 1.3%); the entire stress core (MT1, LCN2, CIRBP, CDKN1A, FKBP5, HIF3A, PDK4, HSPA5/8, XBP1, PMAIP1) is absent.
⇒ ~99% of the microgravity signature is neither reproduced by ground unloading nor explained by radiation/launch — it is microgravity-specific.

4. Mechanistic modules → SANS features

Reproducible + mechanistic candidate genes grouped onto SANS-relevant physiology. Fluid homeostasis and vascular/blood-retinal-barrier are the most SANS-apt; the oxidative/proteostasis/hypoxia core is the systemic spaceflight stress response impinging on the eye.

5. Findings by axis

Per-tissue signature & consensus. 1,203 human-ortholog genes; a 54-gene retina∩optic-nerve core (co-detected in two independent tissues; 42 same-direction) is the strongest reproducibility signal. Within-retina consensus (≥2 of 3 studies, consistent): IRF7↑, SCN4A↑, NR4A3↓, STC1↓. Consistent-direction backbone (≥2 studies) = 49 genes (105 in ≥2 studies overall); the most broadly-detected genes — HSPA8 (4 studies, net down) and PALS1 (3 studies) — are directionally mixed across tissues.
Tissue specificity. 417 systemic · 685 intermediate · 101 eye-selective. The stress core (MT1, LCN2, CIRBP, CDKN1A, IRF7, PDK4, HIF3A) is systemic; the eye-selective fraction is phototransduction (SAG, RCVRN, GUCA1A, GNAT2, CNGB3) plus SST, PMAIP1 — a two-tier "systemic driver × local vulnerability" model.
Fluid / vascular / barrier. Aquaporins ↑ (AQP1 retina, AQP5 optic nerve) — but not AQP4; renin-angiotensin ↑ (REN, AGTR2 — AT2 possibly compensatory); VEGFA ↓ with HIF3A ↑ (atypical HIF represses VEGFA); APLNR ↓, NPPC ↓, NOSTRIN ↑; PALS1 ↑ (Crumbs/BRB polarity).
Disease & phenotype. rdkg: 20 signature genes in the neuro-ocular disease universe (208), over-representation 1.56× (p=0.032, descriptive); PALS1→optic atrophy / retinal detachment; FZD4 (FEVR retinal vascular); GNAT2/CNGB3/GUCA1A cones. spoke-okn: a vascular/hypertension axis (REN, CCL2, CDKN1A, AGTR2 → hypertension/cerebrovascular; LCN2 → CKD; POMT1 → glaucoma).
Countermeasures. spoke-okn drug edges are sparse and its compound→gene layer toxicogenomic; hypotheses are mechanism-derived — renin-angiotensin balance (AT1/AT2), oxidative-stress mitigation (metallothionein/NOXA), vascular/BRB stabilisation, and — most concretely — in-orbit artificial gravity (the 1G-centrifugation control abolishes the signature).

7. Caveats & reproducibility

All eye data are mouse; human relevance is ortholog-inferred (no human ocular spaceflight omics). Per-study n is small (three retina studies carry the informative signal; OSD-194 is essentially null). Microgravity, radiation and launch/housing are partially confounded in flight — addressed with the on-orbit 1G-centrifugation control but not fully separable. The optic nerve has no terrestrial GXA baseline. The KG carries no blood tissue (immune organs substituted). Transcriptomics only. Formal GO/Reactome/AOP enrichment was constrained by bridged (Entrez→HGNC via Wikidata) joins and substituted with direct-join digcfdekg trait enrichment. Over-representation statistics are descriptive (rdkg universe dominated by one broad syndromic disease). Extreme |log2fc|≥10 (near-zero-count artifacts) were flagged and down-weighted. Every SPARQL query (verbatim, graphs hit, row counts) and the detailed discussion + provenance are in SANS_reproducibility_transcript.md; rules/thresholds/joins in SANS_reproducibility_appendix.md.
