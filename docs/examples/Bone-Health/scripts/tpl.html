Bottom line. The federation holds exactly one in-flight bone tissue — bone marrow (OSD-690, flown wild-type and Nrf2-knockout) and no in-flight mineralized-bone omics. The wild-type flight signature (3,161 genes) is a coherent bone-loss program — ALPL↓, IBSP↓, FAM20A↓ (formation/mineralization) with CXCL2↑ (inflammation). The Nrf2-KO arm replicates it (98.4% same direction) and shows that losing the oxidative-stress regulator Nrf2 broadens bone-remodeling collapse (COL1A1, LRP5, NFATC1, CSF1, MMP9 significant only in KO) — nominating antioxidant / Nrf2-activating countermeasures.

Nrf2 axis — internal replicate & oxidative-stress deconfounder

Figure 2. Cross-genotype reproducibility and the Nrf2-dependent bone response (OSD-690 flight bone marrow; mouse-derived, ortholog-inferred). Bone relevance: systemic stress, skeletal vulnerability

Figure 3. Bone relevance of the mouse flight bone-marrow signature (mouse-derived, ortholog-inferred). (A) Bone-loss over-representation. Observed = signature genes that are also known bone-loss genes; expected = the number expected if the 3,021-gene signature were a random gene set of the same size (hypergeometric null; background = 21,052 Entrez-mapped genes); fold = observed ÷ expected. Not enriched against the broad GWAS BMD (bone mineral density) / fracture set (3,412 genes; 1.00×, p=0.46) but enriched against curated Mendelian bone-loss genes (rdkg HPO Osteoporosis/Osteopenia/Reduced-BMD/fracture, 148 genes; 1.46×, p=0.018). (B) Tissue specificity. Each of the 221 high-effect genes classed by how many of the 33 other spaceflight (Space-Flight-vs-Ground) tissues it is also significant in: systemic = ≥3 tissues (shared, organism-wide spaceflight stress), intermediate = 1–2, marrow-selective = 0 (changed only in bone marrow). (C) Canonical bone-remodeling genes. Established bone genes read directly from the OSD-690 flight bone-marrow assays (spoke-genelab MEASURED_DIFFERENTIAL_EXPRESSION_ASmMG), wild-type and Nrf2-KO arms; values are log2FC (Space Flight vs Ground; blue = down in flight, red = up); ns = not significant / not in that arm’s tested set. Formation/matrix genes (ALPL, IBSP, FAM20A, COL1A1) fall; osteoclast/Wnt genes (NFATC1, LRP5, CSF1, MMP9) fall only when Nrf2 is lost; inflammation (CXCL2) rises in both.
Interpretation of the over-representation analysis (Figure 3A). Panel A tests whether the flight bone-marrow signature is preferentially enriched for bone genes, or whether its overlap with bone-gene sets is no greater than expected for a gene list of its size. The GWAS BMD/fracture set (left) is broad and permissive (~3,412 genes, ~16% of all protein-coding genes), so a ~3,000-gene signature overlaps it at almost exactly the chance rate (492 vs 490 expected; 1.00×, p=0.46) — a null that reflects the set’s size, not an absence of bone biology. The curated Mendelian bone-loss set (right) is a small, high-penetrance set of 148 genes whose mutation causes monogenic osteoporosis / osteopenia / reduced-BMD / fracture, and here the signature carries 1.46× more than expected (31 vs 21; p=0.018). Together they show the signature’s bone relevance is concentrated in high-penetrance, causal skeletal genes, not the diffuse polygenic background — and those 31 overlapping genes are the ones topping the ranked list (ALPL, ANKH, LRP5, IBSP, GATA1, IFT140, CLCN3…). Caveats: the effect is modest and descriptive (one hypergeometric test, 31 observed genes; enrichment ≠ causation) and mouse-derived, ortholog-inferred. Figure 3A sharpens the bone hypothesis rather than proving it — it is the statistical warrant for treating the skeletal-disease genes in the signature as a genuine, prioritisable core rather than coincidence.

Unloading (disuse) attribution — a data gap

Hindlimb unloading is the gold-standard disuse analog and is present directly in bone, but is uninformative in this release: cortical-bone HLU = 8 genes, marrow HLU = near-zero-count artefacts. The microgravity-vs-disuse-vs-radiation decomposition cannot be estimated here — a structural limitation, not a negative result.

Countermeasure / target hypotheses

Mechanism-derived research hypotheses — not medical advice. spoke-okn has no usable bone drug edges, so these are inferred from the gene-level signature + GO pathways; every human-level link is mouse-derived / ortholog-inferred.
__CM__
Best-supported: Nrf2 activation / antioxidants (backed by both the genetic Nrf2-KO result and the enriched oxidative-stress / OXPHOS GO programs) and mechanical loading / resistive exercise (the established in-flight countermeasure opposing the disuse state the signature reflects). Wnt re-activation (anti-sclerostin), PTH anabolics and anti-resorptives map onto the down-regulated formation and osteoclast genes.

Retrieved curated drugs (rdkg)

Looking past spoke-okn (no osteoporosis TREATS edges) and prokn (medicinal-chemistry probes, not named drugs), rdkg's treats layer returns the established anti-osteoporosis armamentarium and maps cleanly onto the dysregulated signature genes. Curated human-disease drugs — not spaceflight-validated, not medical advice.
__DRUGS__
Striking alignments: Asfotase alfa (recombinant alkaline phosphatase, hypophosphatasia) directly correlates with flight ALPL↓; romosozumab (anti-sclerostin) engages the down-regulated LRP5/WIF1 Wnt axis and is indicated for osteogenesis imperfecta (COL1A1); denosumab/bisphosphonates target the osteoclast program (NFATC1/CSF1/MMP9/CA2); teriparatide/PTH drives the suppressed osteoblast-formation program (ALPL/IBSP/COL1A1); rdkg even links bisphosphonates to osteoporosis-pseudoglioma (LRP5) and OI (COL1A1) — the exact Tier-A Mendelian genes.

Comparison with the published literature

Each principal finding was checked against the primary literature using PubMed and the Paperclip full-text corpus (six central claims verified against full text). According to PubMed and the Paperclip corpus, the convergence is strong; one directional discrepancy is flagged as a testable prediction.
Bone loss & osteoblast suppression. Astronauts lose >10% areal BMD over ~6 months [1]; spaceflight compromises bone via reduced formation, elevated resorption and impaired mineralization [2] — matching the flight signature (ALPL, IBSP, FAM20A, COL1A1 down).
Oxidative stress & Nrf2 (the novel node). Oxidative stress / redox signalling is an established driver of spaceflight skeletal loss [3][4], and disuse-induced mitochondrial/oxidative stress is buffered by Nrf2 [5] — matching the enriched OXPHOS and oxidative-stress programs (Figs 4–5). Nrf2 deficiency causes bone loss [6] and promotes ROS-driven osteoclastogenesis [7][8]; and a different knockout (hepcidin) likewise worsens unloading bone loss [9] — the same “KO worsens unloading” logic as the OSD-690 Nrf2-KO arm.
One directional discrepancy (testable). Terrestrial RANKL models predict Nrf2 loss increases NFATc1-driven osteoclastogenesis [7][8], yet the whole-marrow spaceflight snapshot shows the osteoclast program (NFATC1/CSF1/MMP9) down in Nrf2-KO — likely a bulk-tissue global-remodeling suppression rather than a directed differentiation readout; worth resolving with sorted-cell / functional assays.
Inflammation, Wnt/sclerostin, countermeasures. Marrow CXCL2/CCL2 fit spaceflight “inflammaging” (NF-κB) [10]; the down-regulated LRP5/WIF1 Wnt axis and anti-sclerostin rationale match osteocyte-mechanosensing / SOST biology [11]. The top lead — antioxidant / Nrf2 activation — is supported by Trolox in microgravity osteoblasts [12], Nrf2 activators inhibiting osteoclastogenesis [8], and resveratrol [13] / pinoresinol [14] in disuse/HLU; retrieved drugs also find footing (eldecalcitol in disuse [15]; anti-sclerostin / PTH / bisphosphonate / denosumab standard of care).
What this study adds: an integrative knowledge-graph meta-analysis that surfaces an Nrf2-gated bone-remodeling set from an in-flight WT-vs-Nrf2-KO contrast and anchors the marrow signature to specific Mendelian/druggable genes (ALPL, LRP5, ANKH) and matching agents (asfotase alfa, romosozumab). No finding contradicts the literature; it concentrates and gene-anchors mechanisms described piecemeal, yielding one falsifiable discrepancy.

Caveats & reproducibility

Mouse-only, ortholog-inferred. Only in-flight bone tissue is marrow (not mineralized bone). Reproducibility is cross-genotype (WT vs Nrf2-KO), not cross-study. Unloading attribution impossible in this release. Signature mostly systemic; enrichment descriptive. No usable bone drug layer. Transcriptomics only.
