2. Source map — Maine PFAS detections vs. co-located facilities

Each marker is an EPA PFAS-relevant facility (fiokg / EPA FRS) that shares a ~1 km S2 Level-13 cell with one or more SAWGraph PFAS samples. Colour = source category (from facility name / NAICS); size = the peak single-compound PFAS concentration in that ~1 km cell (log scale, ng/L, water). Basemap: OpenStreetMap tiles (© OpenStreetMap contributors). Where tiles cannot load — e.g. the in-app sandbox — the map falls back automatically to SpatialKG county geometry. Every marker is KG-derived.

The strongest signals recover Maine's principal PFAS-source sites purely from graph structure: military airfields with AFFF firefighting-foam history (Brunswick NAS, Bangor Air National Guard, former Loring AFB, NSA Cutler, Portsmouth Naval Shipyard), pulp & paper mills (Androscoggin / Verso · Pixelle, Woodland, Great Northern), municipal landfills and wastewater-treatment facilities, and textile / tannery mills.

4. Candidate source categories (NAICS)

Industry classes of the PFAS-relevant facilities co-located with Maine PFAS samples — a data-driven ranking of candidate source sectors. These are the industry sectors flagged by EPA's PFAS Analytic Tools — the basis of fiokg's EPA-PFAS-Facility class — so the prioritisation is internally consistent with the knowledge graph's own source designation.

5. Detected chemicals — identity, use & toxicology coverage

Every PFAS analyte detected in Maine co-located cells, resolved to its CompTox DTXSID, with ICE functional-use records and ToxCast high-throughput assay coverage joined on DTXSID. Concentrations are peak ng/L in water media.

Methodological note: joining on the DTXSID that SAWGraph natively carries recovers 35 chemicals with ICE records and 33 with ToxCast coverage — far more than the CAS-based crosswalk (12 / 7), because Maine EGAD stores CAS without dashes, breaking the identifiers.org/cas join. The KG's native DTXSID is the more complete bridge.
