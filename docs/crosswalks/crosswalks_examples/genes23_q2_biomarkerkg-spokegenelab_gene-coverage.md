**Why the two numbers matter separately.** Presence in a graph is cheap; presence *with data* is what makes a join usable. Here they are one apart, so effectively every clinical biomarker gene has been measured in at least one spaceflight assay. That makes this the most complete of BiomarkerKG's four Entrez joins for downstream analysis — no attrition to plan around.

**The reason coverage is near-total** is that GeneLab's assays are transcriptome-wide: an array or RNA-seq panel measures the whole expressed genome, so any well-known clinical marker gene is on it. That is also the caveat: near-total coverage here reflects platform breadth, not that spaceflight *changes* these genes. Establishing change requires clean Space Flight vs Ground Control contrasts with matched covariates (`get_valid_contrasts`), not the presence of a measurement.

**Query-shape note.** As in G22-Q2, an `OPTIONAL` carrying the BIND-constructed gene IRI into the remote graph returned a false **0** for the differential-expression count; it has to be a separate query with the pattern required, not optional.
