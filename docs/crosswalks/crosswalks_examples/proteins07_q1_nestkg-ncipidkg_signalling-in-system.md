**Why this answers the question.** Every high-evidence pair lands in a NeST system whose *name* describes the very signalling event NCI-PID asserts, and the two graphs derived those independently — NCI-PID from curated literature, NeST from protein-interaction and proteomics data:

- **TP53 → CDKN1A** (2,324 evidence records, the strongest edge in the set) sits inside 10 NeST systems at once, including "TP53 regulates transcription of cell cycle genes", "Cell cycle arrest" and "G1 to S cell cycle control". The nesting is the point: p53's induction of p21 is not confined to one assembly, it is the hinge between several.
- **TP53 ↔ MDM4** and **MDM4 ⊣ TP53** both fall in "MDM2-p53 pathway" — the curated inhibitory edge and the data-derived complex naming the same axis.
- **MTOR → ULK1** falls in "Regulatory in autophagy via inhibition of mTORC1"; **TGFBR1 → SMAD2** in "SMAD-TGFbeta signaling"; **INSR → IRS1** in "EGFR-insulin receptor cross-talk". Each is the textbook first step of the pathway its NeST system is named for.
- **IL6 ↔ IL10** appears with both signs (IL6 positively regulating IL10, IL10 negatively regulating IL6) inside "Regulation of interleukin production" — a feedback loop reconstructed from two directed statements.

**What the join buys.** NeST tells you a set of proteins is one assembly under mutational selection but not who acts on whom; NCI-PID tells you who acts on whom but not which assembly it belongs to. Joined, you get *directed, evidence-weighted signalling within a named cancer system* — the shape you need to ask which edge of a mutated system is the actionable one.

**Two reading caveats.** (1) Opposite-sign statements coexist for the same pair (TP53 both positively and negatively regulating CDKN1A) because INDRA aggregates the literature, including context-dependent and contradictory reports; the evidence counts, not the sign alone, carry the weight. (2) `sharedSystems` counts NeST's *nested* systems, so one interaction can legitimately be inside ten of them — that is containment, not ten independent confirmations.
