Yet securechainkg's manufacturer directory has no firm classified there, so it is the one device industry for which this join can say nothing about the hardware supply base.

**Two more are barely covered.** In-vitro diagnostics and dental laboratories each rest on a single securechainkg manufacturer, so anything said about those industries through this join is really a statement about one firm.

**Where coverage is real.** Eight industries have 13 or more securechainkg manufacturers, led by pharmaceutical preparation (47), surgical and medical instruments (39) and precision turned products (34). In those industries an industry-level comparison between FDA's roster and securechainkg's suppliers has enough on both sides to mean something.

**Compared with the other industry partner.** The same 13 codes all match in fiokg (I08-Q2), EPA's facility registry, which covers the whole regulated economy. securechainkg is a much smaller manufacturer directory, so 12 of 13 is close to the most it could offer.
