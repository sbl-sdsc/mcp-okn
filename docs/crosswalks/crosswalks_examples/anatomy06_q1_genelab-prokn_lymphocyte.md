**Three changes, and the first two are corrections of method.**

**1. The comparability rule now comes from the server, not from the query.** The previous version implemented GeneLab's contrast rule as two *nested* `FILTER NOT EXISTS` blocks over the factor arrays. That construct is what QLever fails to plan, and — as the companion Gammaproteobacteria transcript showed — it is not reliable: there, three assays passed the hand-written filter but only one was genuinely clean. **Why this answers the question.** Fifteen canonical lymphocyte markers are significantly dysregulated in spaceflight immune tissue, and the pattern is not uniform — it splits by direction in a way that maps onto lymphocyte biology.

**Up in spaceflight:** `Il7r` (the IL-7 receptor driving T-cell homeostatic survival, the strongest signal at p = 7e−7), `Ltb` (lymphotoxin-β, lymphoid tissue organisation), `Cd3d` and `Cd247` (TCR complex chains), and a block of NK/cytotoxic-granule genes — `Nkg7`, `Klrd1` (CD94), `Cst7`, `Cmc1`.

**Down in spaceflight:** `Cd69` — the canonical early lymphocyte **activation** marker, and the most broadly measured gene here (3 assays) — together with `Gzmb` (granzyme B, the cytotoxic effector), `Txk` (a T-cell kinase), `Cd8a`, and the B-cell gene `Zcchc7`.

That combination is the interesting part: markers of lymphocyte *identity and receptor machinery* rise while markers of *activation and cytotoxic effector function* fall. `Klrd1` and `Nkg7` up alongside `Gzmb` down within the NK panel is the same dissociation inside one cell type — the cells are present and phenotypically NK, but their effector programme is damped. That is consistent with the long-standing observation that spaceflight impairs immune function without eliminating lymphocytes, and it is exactly the sort of split a single-marker analysis would miss.

**Cautions.** Effect sizes are modest (mostly |log2FC| < 1.5) and several rest on a **single** clean assay, so the per-gene evidence is uneven — `Cd69`, `Aoah` (3 assays) and the six two-assay genes are the better-supported rows. The mouse-symbol mapping is mechanical capitalisation of the human symbol, which is correct for the overwhelming majority of orthologues but is a convention, not a curated mapping; genes whose mouse orthologue is named differently would be missed silently. And 36 of the 51 ProKN markers returned nothing — absence here means "not significant in these ten assays", not "unchanged in spaceflight".
