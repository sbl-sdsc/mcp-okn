Three leaves, three completely different results — and the largest number is the least informative.

**fiokg: 19.8% is real co-location.** ufokn's urban flood footprint and the EPA facility registry are independent national datasets, and about one in five flood cells contains a regulated facility. That is a substantive finding about the world.

**sawgraph: 0 is a real absence, exactly.** The earlier run got 0 from a sample and had to record it as "the sampled region does not reach this leaf" — a hedge, since a sample cannot distinguish absence from bad luck. The exact query settles it: **zero of 97,087**. sawgraph carries 88,007 S2 cells of its own, so it is not empty; it is Maine-focused, and ufokn's urban flood layer simply does not cover Maine. The two graphs are genuinely disjoint in space.

**hydrologykg: 100% is an artefact, not a discovery.** Every single ufokn cell appears in hydrologykg — which sounds like perfect agreement and is nothing of the kind. hydrologykg carries **7,404,649** distinct S2 Level-13 cells on `owl:sameAs`: it is a **national S2 index**, roughly 76× ufokn's entire cell set. Any collection of US S2 cells will be almost entirely contained in it. The join succeeds for every row because containment is guaranteed by construction, so it tells you nothing about whether flood risk and hydrology features actually coincide.

**So: which leaf-to-leaf joins are verifiable?** The question conceals a trap. All three joins *execute*, and a naive reading would rank hydrologykg as the best partner and sawgraph as a failure. The truth inverts that:

- **fiokg is the only informative join** — a partial overlap between independently-scoped datasets, where the 19.8% is a measurement.
- **sawgraph is a verified non-join** — useful precisely because 0 is now exact rather than sampled, so it can be recorded as a known non-overlap instead of being retried.
- **hydrologykg is a spurious join** — 100% success carrying no information. Reporting "ufokn co-locates with hydrologykg on 97,087 cells" would be true and misleading.

**The general lesson.** When one side of a spatial join is an index rather than an observation layer, overlap fraction stops being evidence. Before quoting a co-location rate, check the partner's own cardinality: 19,252 out of fiokg's 681,045 cells is selective; 97,087 out of hydrologykg's 7.4M is what containment looks like. A high match rate against a large index is the spatial equivalent of joining against a lookup table and calling it agreement.
