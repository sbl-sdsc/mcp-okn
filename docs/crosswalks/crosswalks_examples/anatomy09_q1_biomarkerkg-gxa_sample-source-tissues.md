BiomarkerKG's `obo:OBCI_1000018` records the anatomical entity a biomarker is *sampled from*; GXA's `biolink:has_attribute` records the tissue an assay was *performed on*. Research question: for the tissue a biomarker is sampled from, what expression data does GXA hold for the same tissue?

**Why this answers the question, and what the two columns really contrast.** The ordering exposes a structural difference between clinical measurement and transcriptomics that this join makes visible in one table:

- **Fluids are where biomarkers live, and where GXA is silent.** Blood plasma: 477 biomarkers, **1** GXA assay. Cerebrospinal fluid: 77 vs 1. Amniotic fluid 83 vs 3, peritoneal fluid 36 vs 4, synovial fluid 22 vs 4. Clinical biomarkers are overwhelmingly measured in accessible body fluids; expression atlases profile *tissues*, because fluids have little resident transcriptome to sequence.
- **Solid organs invert the ratio.** Lung: 32 biomarkers, **483** assays. Kidney 33 vs 201, pancreas 45 vs 102, ovary 36 vs 48. GXA is rich exactly where sampling is invasive and biomarkers are scarce.
- **Whole blood is the one place both are deep** — 734 biomarkers and 674 assays — which makes it the tissue where this crosswalk actually supports a joint analysis.

**What the join buys.** It tells you, before you design anything, whether a biomarker's sample site has transcriptomic support. A plasma marker has essentially none; a lung or kidney marker has hundreds of assays to draw on. That is a planning answer neither graph gives alone.

**Caveats.** (1) "Biomarkers sampled here" counts BiomarkerKG assertions, so a single well-studied analyte contributes many. (2) Sample source and assay tissue are related but not identical concepts — a plasma protein originates somewhere else. (3) `blood` and `venous blood` are separate UBERON terms and are not merged here.

PREFIX obo: <http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/>
PREFIX rdfs: <http://www.w3.org/2000/01/rdf-schema#>

SELECT ?tissueLabel (COUNT(DISTINCT ?biomarker) AS ?biomarkersSampledHere)
       (COUNT(DISTINCT ?gxaAssay) AS ?gxaAssays) WHERE {
  GRAPH <https://purl.org/okn/frink/kg/biomarkerkg> { ?biomarker obo:OBCI_1000018 ?ub . }
  GRAPH <https://purl.org/okn/frink/kg/gene-expression-atlas-okn> { ?gxaAssay blv:has_attribute ?ub . }
  GRAPH <https://purl.org/okn/frink/kg/ubergraph> { ?ub rdfs:label ?tissueLabel }
} GROUP BY ?tissueLabel ORDER BY DESC(?biomarkersSampledHere) LIMIT 15
