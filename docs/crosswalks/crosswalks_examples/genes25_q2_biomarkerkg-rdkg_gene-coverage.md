**167 of 201 — 83.1%.** The lowest coverage of BiomarkerKG's four Entrez partners, and the most informative one.

| Measure | Count |
|---|---|
| BiomarkerKG gene analytes | 201 |
| … present in RDKG | 167 (83.1%) |
| … with at least one RDKG ClinVar variant (`has_gene`) | **6** |
| ClinVar variants on those 6 genes | 1,665 |

**Two coverage numbers, two very different meanings.** 167 is how many biomarker genes RDKG has *heard of* — mostly through broad `related_to` association edges. **6** is how many have catalogued pathogenic variation attached. The gap between 167 and 6 is the whole point of this crosswalk: RDKG's association layer is wide and shallow, its variant layer narrow and specific.

And the 6 genes that do carry variants carry a lot of them — 1,665 ClinVar variants between them, an average of nearly 280 each. Those are the genes where a clinical biomarker assay and rare-disease genetics genuinely meet.

**Why 83.1% and not 99%.** RDKG is rare-disease scoped, so a purely inflammatory or metabolic marker with no rare-disease literature simply is not in it. Compare spoke-genelab and digcfdekg at 200/201 (genome-wide catalogues) and SPOKE at 191/201.

**Query-shape note.** The `OPTIONAL` form of the variant count returned a false **0** — the same BIND-injected-IRI-into-a-remote-OPTIONAL trap seen in G22-Q2 and G23-Q2. Both figures here come from separate queries with the pattern required rather than optional.
