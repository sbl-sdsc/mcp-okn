The MONDO figure matches this crosswalk's stored `verified_count` of 601 exactly. The DOID route survives only as a **vestige**: those 6 diseases are still attached to real biomarker predicates (`obo:OBCI_1000002` diagnostic-for on 2, `obo:OBCI_1000008` indicates-risk-of on 4), so they are not pure orphans — but they are what remains of the 28-DOID remnant left behind by the migration.

The 2026-06-18 version of this transcript joined on DOID **and** required a `spoke-okn` label for the same DOID IRI. That three-way requirement is what took it from 6 to **0 rows**, so it failed silently while claiming "54 shared diseases". Labels now come from ubergraph, which carries them for MONDO.

**Why the join is required.** BiomarkerKG holds the literature biomarker records but no dataset inventory; NDE holds the NIAID dataset inventory but no biomarker evidence. Neither can answer "which biomarker-tracked diseases have data available to study them" alone.

**Why this answers the question.** Every disease listed has both a curated BiomarkerKG biomarker record and a substantial NDE dataset holding — these are the conditions where a literature-nominated marker could actually be tested against available data. The mix is exactly what NIAID's remit predicts once oncology is included: immune-mediated disease (rheumatoid arthritis), infectious disease (COVID-19), neurodegeneration (Alzheimer) and a broad haematological/solid-tumour spread.

**Read the counts as coverage, not priority.** `cancer` at 17,374 and `carcinoma` at 1,919 are high-level MONDO terms that absorb everything beneath them, so the top of this table is partly an artefact of ontology granularity rather than of research volume. The specific terms — prostate cancer, AML, hepatocellular carcinoma — are the ones that support a concrete follow-up query.

**Scale correction worth recording.** The retired DOID route reported 54 shared diseases in 2026-06-18. The live MONDO route finds **601** — an order of magnitude more. Anyone who took the old figure as the size of this crosswalk understated it roughly elevenfold, and the crosswalk's own example question carried that stale 54 until it was corrected alongside this transcript.
