The triples are not redundancy in the join — Complex Portal enumerates variant compositions of the same assembly as separate CPX accessions, so a 14-3-3 or cyclin–CDK family legitimately appears as several near-overlapping entries.

**Does the shared-complex structure recover known biology? NCI-PID says two proteins interact; ProKN says they belong to one machine; neither graph contains the other's claim.
