**The ratio is the story: 3,479 traits from 200 genes**, roughly 17 traits per gene. CFDE REVEAL is an inference engine over large-scale data, so its gene→trait edges are abundant and pleiotropic by design. Compare the other side: BiomarkerKG ties each analyte to a small number of curated marker roles. The join therefore pairs a *narrow, curated* claim with a *broad, inferred* one — useful for corroboration, dangerous if the inferred breadth is read as clinical breadth.

**How this compares across BiomarkerKG's Entrez partners.** digcfdekg and spoke-genelab both reach 200 of 201; SPOKE reaches 191 and rdkg 167. digcfdekg is the one that comes with the richest per-gene payload, which makes it the natural partner when the question is "what else is this marker gene implicated in?" rather than "is it in the graph?".

**Caveat.** 100% trait coverage of the matched genes does not mean every marker has a *relevant* inferred trait — it means CFDE has an opinion about all of them. Filter by trait before drawing a conclusion.
